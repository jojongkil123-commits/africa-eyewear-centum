How to find us

  1. Take Busan Metro Line 2 to Centum City Station.
  2. Head toward Exit 10 — the store is right below it, on B2 of the department store (about 55 m).
  3. We face the B2 central plaza, on the passage between the department store and the mall.
  4. Spa Land is in the same Shinsegae Centum City complex.

What we do

  • Free eye test by licensed opticians — walk in, no appointment
  • Single-vision prescription glasses ready in about 30 minutes
  • Prescription lenses and frame fitting
  • Authentic designer sunglasses and frames
  • Tax refund for foreign visitors

Frequently asked questions

Can I get prescription glasses the same day in Busan?

Yes. Single-vision prescription glasses are usually ready in about 30 minutes. Some special lenses can take longer — please ask our staff.

Do I need an appointment for an eye test?

No. The eye test is free and you can just walk in. It is done by licensed opticians before we make your glasses.

Can I get a tax refund (VAT refund)?

Yes. Foreign visitors can get an instant tax refund at the counter, or receive a tax-refund receipt to claim at the department store's refund kiosk or at the airport. Please bring your passport.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, UnionPay, Alipay and WeChat Pay. Prices are in Korean won (KRW).

Do you speak English?

Yes, our staff can help you in English. For other languages we use a translation app.

How much do glasses cost?

It depends on the frame and lenses you choose. Please ask in store — we will show you options for your budget.

Where exactly is the store?

On B2 of Shinsegae Centum City, right below Exit 10 of Centum City Station (Line 2), facing the B2 central plaza between the department store and the mall.

What are your opening hours?

Mon–Thu 10:30–20:00; Fri–Sun and public holidays 10:30–20:30. We are closed on the department store's closing days (2026: Oct 19 and Nov 9).

Which brands do you carry?

Oakley, Ray-Ban, Miu Miu, Moscot, 999.9, Kamemannen, Masunaga, Eyevan 7285, Markus T, Lash, Laurence Paul, Public Beacon, plus our own house brands.
